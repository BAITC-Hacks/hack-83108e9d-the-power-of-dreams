## Purpose

Provide a reproducible local contractor-selection demonstration and accurate evidence of integrated quality, performance and publication.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Reproducible documented demonstration
The delivery SHALL document actual installation, configuration, build, launch, primary inputs, expected results, material provenance and limitations. A clean committed source revision SHALL reproduce the primary scenario without hidden author files or a personal browser session; live AI SHALL require explicitly supplied private configuration, funded model access and network.

#### Scenario: Organizer follows the documented local path
- **WHEN** the documented prerequisites and optional private OpenAI configuration are supplied to a clean checkout
- **THEN** installation, build and production launch succeed and the primary scenario renders the expected ordered cards
- **AND** the documentation distinguishes starting prices/calendar marks from confirmed bookings and live AI from fallback.

### Requirement: Final integrated acceptance evidence
The delivery SHALL identify evidence for real dense, rare, both empty outcomes, restart stability, busy venue exclusion, optional filters and both date pairs, plus controlled failure/mixed/local/configuration, zero calls for empty or unavailable configuration, cancellation/stale responses and errors. Reuse SHALL be tied to unchanged code and exact revisions.

#### Scenario: Frozen candidate is evaluated
- **WHEN** a candidate is prepared for publication
- **THEN** its build/type checks, real HTTP/UI outcomes and controlled cases have supported evidence with failures and skipped checks explicitly recorded.

### Requirement: Live rendered quality and timing
The delivery SHALL record three uncached real browser submissions including the first after production startup, measuring submission through visible completed result against the under-ten-second target. Dense and rare live cards SHALL receive per-card source, relevance, facts/one-to-two-sentence length and applicable dense distinctiveness decisions with concrete reasons. Fallback SHALL NOT count as live quality or latency evidence.

#### Scenario: Dense rare dense timing series
- **WHEN** the canonical dense, rare and dense requests are submitted to the real backend with private live configuration
- **THEN** all four required profile IDs have explicit pass/fail/not_run quality decisions, dataset/model/source revision metadata and all three visible-result durations
- **AND** failures remain visible rather than being silently replaced by selected retries.

### Requirement: Truthful publication and submission status
The delivery SHALL distinguish branch publication, verified candidate, local main integration, confirmed remote main publication, archive state and organizer submission. Shared integration SHALL have one recorded owner.

#### Scenario: Accepted delivery is published
- **WHEN** the accepted candidate is promoted without bypassing protection or overwriting another contributor
- **THEN** remote main contains the verified candidate before feature completion is reported
- **AND** organizer submission remains unsubmitted unless explicitly requested and actually performed.
